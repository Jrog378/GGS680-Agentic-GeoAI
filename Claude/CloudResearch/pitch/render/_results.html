03 / 03

These are prototype results on Hurricane Ida development data. The flood detector calls 78.6 percent of 798 roads right in areas it never trained on, against 55.3 percent for always guessing flooded; we score in held-out areas because splitting by tile overstated accuracy by about 4 points. Our cloud spotter reaches an F1 of 0.87 against expert cloud masks, against 0.65 for the standard dark-channel method, better on 112 of 120 images. Features from Meta's DINOv2 foundation model add 1.3 points, in every one of 21 fold splits, and a newer model, DINOv3, reaches 83 percent as a research benchmark, so there is room to grow. The limits are honest ones: one event, one sensor, roads only; the de-hazing gain under thin cloud is not yet significant; and the current models are research-only because of the training data's licences. Next: a second disaster, licence-clean training data, then one final test on areas that have never been scored.
