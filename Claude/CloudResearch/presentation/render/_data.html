The test case is Hurricane Ida in Louisiana: Maxar before and after images at half a metre, and 1,037 OpenStreetMap roads with SpaceNet 8 flood labels, on development tiles only. The test tiles and the other areas have never been scored. Two quality issues matter: flood labels are yes or missing, never no, and all the real cloud sits in one after image.
