The second result is the two-date idea. The clear before image tells us how dark the ground's darkest pixels should be, so how much they brightened measures the haze, and we correct only inside the cloud mask. On synthetic haze, He's method, which does not use the before image, gets closer on brightness but changes most of the clear ground; ours keeps the detail and leaves clear ground alone. This test favours our method, because the fake haze follows our model. On this tile little road detail comes back.
