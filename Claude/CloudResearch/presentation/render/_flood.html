Then the flood calls, each test written down before it ran. Change is always found by comparing the before and after images; only the de-hazing differs. Under thin cloud, two-date de-hazing finds 20 of the 24 flooded roads and He's de-hazing 22, against 17 with none; neither gain is significant, and clear roads stay near 78 percent. Under clear sky on the same tiles, it finds 85 percent of flooded roads. So far our two-date method does not beat the baseline on flood calls. To show a gain counts we need more flooded roads, from a second event, and a cleaner cloud edge. These roads were seen before, so this is exploratory.
