Proposed method: detect, handle, report

Change detector

Random forest (Breiman 2001) on classical, texture (Haralick 1979) and DINOv2 (Oquab et al. 2024) cues: 79% on 798 roads in new areas (always “flooded”: 55.3%).

Baselines and checks

Tile cloud rule; no de-hazing; He 2011. Folds blocked by 1.75 km area (Roberts et al. 2017), McNemar tests (McNemar 1947); a second event as the final test.

Agents propose, I decide

Question, critic and validation agents draft and check; the handle gate is coded rules. Decided by hand: the problem, the questions, the cloud rule.
