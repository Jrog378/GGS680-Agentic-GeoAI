05 / 15

The first result is about the labels. Under cloud, far fewer roads carry a flood label: 55 percent of clear roads, 26 percent under thin cloud, 2 percent on opaque tiles. Part of that is geography, but analysts only labelled what they could see, so for this dataset a flooded road under cloud reads as dry. On this tile every flood label sits on the clear side. A model scored on these labels is rewarded for calling cloud dry.
