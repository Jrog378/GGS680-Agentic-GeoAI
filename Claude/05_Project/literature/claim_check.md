# Claim check: deck claims vs. full-text PDFs

Checked 2026-10-04 against local full-text PDFs (pypdf extraction). Page numbers are PDF page indices.

## Claim verdicts

| # | Paper | Deck claim | Verdict | PDF p. | Verbatim quote (<=25 words) | Correction / note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1a | Xuan et al. 2025 (DynamicVL) | "best of 18 models: 34.1% on long-term city-change questions" | SUPPORTED (minor wording) | 3, 8 | "We evaluate 18 vision-language models ... the best-performing model, o4-mini, achieves only 34.1% accuracy on DVL-Bench's overall QA average" | 34.1% is the overall multiple-choice QA average on DVL-Bench, not one question type. Better wording: "34.1% average QA accuracy on DVL-Bench (o4-mini)". |
| 1b | Xuan et al. 2025 | "1 m NAIP imagery, 42 U.S. cities, 2005–2023" | SUPPORTED | 1, 4 | "high-resolution (1.0m GSD) remote sensing imagery from the National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP), covering 42 major U.S. cities" / "from 2005 to 2023" | none |
| 2 | Kao et al. 2026 (ACL Findings) | "40% zero-shot, about 60% with self-reflection; generated code failed over 44% of the time" | SUPPORTED | 1 | "LLM agents achieve an accuracy of 40.0% where the code fails to run over 44% of the time" ... "accuracies rise to around 60%" | The ~60% applies to Claude-4.5-Sonnet, Gemini-2.5-Pro and GPT-5 with a reflexion framework. The paper adds that this is "only marginally above random chance". |
| 3 | Irvin et al. 2025 (TEOChat) | "temporal vision-language models see at most 8 images" | PARTLY | 5, 6 | "Due to memory constraints, we limit the sequences to a maximum of 8 images, and randomly sample 8 images without replacement from longer sequences." | The cap is TEOChat's own limit, set by memory (one 16 GB A4000). Longer sequences are randomly subsampled to 8. It is not a limit for temporal VLMs in general. Correct to: "TEOChat handles at most 8 images per sequence". The Video-LLaVA video encoder also takes fixed 8-frame inputs (p6). |
| 4a | Feng et al. 2026 (Earth-Agent, ICLR) | "Tool-using EO agent (Earth-Agent, 104 tools)" | SUPPORTED | 3, 5 | "Earth-Agent integrates 104 specialized tools organized into five functional kits." | none |
| 4b | Feng et al. 2026 | "SAR and panchromatic data are rarely tested" | SUPPORTED (consistent) | 2, 6 | "The benchmark integrates three major types of Earth observation data: RGB Imagery (RGB), Raw Spectral Data (Spectrum), and Processed Earth Products (Products)." | Earth-Bench has no SAR or panchromatic modality. "SAR" appears once in the paper, in the intro (p2), as a modality MLLMs "cannot process". |
| 5 | Lee et al. 2026 (JSTARS) | "SAR time-series change types; up-steps linked to construction" | SUPPORTED | 2, 3 | "a step change could represent either permanent building construction (upward backscatter increase) or land clearing (downward backscatter decrease)" | The classes are up step, down step, spike/long impulse, regular/irregular cycle, complex and unchanged, from Sentinel-1 (p3). |
| 6a | Qu et al. 2026 (AnyTime-CD, RSE) | "Dense time-series change detection; AOI-level split; misalignment creates false change" | SUPPORTED | 4, 7, 12 | "partition the dataset into training, validation, and test subsets at a 4:1:1 ratio based on independent AOIs" / "false positives induced by illumination or registration noise" | Misalignment: p7 says it causes "noise in model training". p2 says pixel misalignment persists even after geometric alignment. |
| 6b | Qu et al. 2026 | "MUDS (SpaceNet 7) and DynamicEarthNet ... public with per-time-point labels" | PARTLY | 3, 4 | "two public and diverse datasets: MUDS and DynamicEarthNet" / "imagery acquired daily ... land-cover annotations on the first day of each month" | MUDS has labels "for each time point" (p3). DynamicEarthNet imagery is daily but its labels are monthly (first day of each month). Say "monthly labels" for DynamicEarthNet. |
| 7 | Zuo et al. 2026 (JSTARS) | "Statistical calibration reduces hallucinated change claims" / "hallucinated change is a recognised failure" | PARTLY | 1, 2 | "to address the hallucination issues commonly associated with LLMs, we introduce a CVA-based coordinations iterative calibration loop" | The failure mode is supported. The method is not described as "statistical calibration". It is a change-vector-analysis (CVA) iterative calibration loop that corrects change coordinates, plus self-consistency voting over reasoning paths. Reword to "CVA-based iterative calibration (+ self-consistency)". |
| 8 | Soni et al. 2025 (EarthDial, CVPR) | instruction data covers RGB, SAR, multispectral; supports bi-/multi-temporal tasks | SUPPORTED | 1 | "over 11.11M instruction pairs covering RGB, Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR), and multispectral modalities ... handles bi-temporal and multi-temporal sequence analysis" | none |
| 9 | Hou et al. 2026 (SAR FM review, SCIS) | SAR all-weather; layover/foreshortening/shadow; "multimodal SAR agents" as future direction | SUPPORTED | 1, 3 | "SAR provides all-weather, day-and-night observation" / "produces foreshortening, layover, and radar shadow" / "future directions covering dataset construction, multimodal SAR agents" | none |
| 10 | Michel & Inglada 2026 (TAMRF, RSE) | band-sharpening relies on a scale-invariance hypothesis that studies show is unrealistic | SUPPORTED | 3 | "Several studies show that the scale invariance hypothesis is unrealistic and can lead to incorrect estimation of local textures" / "Yet it relies on the unrealistic scale invariance hypothesis" | Citation year: RSE vol. 334 (2026), but available online 11 Dec 2025, which is why the file is named rse.2025. Either year is defensible; use 2026 consistently. |
| 11 | Deshmukh et al. 2023 (MuRA-T, arXiv) | Landsat-8 (incl. 15 m pan) + Sentinel-2 stacks for the 60 SpaceNet 7 training AOIs; ~24 monthly images/AOI; least-cloud scene per month; sub-pixel alignment | SUPPORTED (pan caveat) | 1, 3, 4, 2 | "temporally and spatially aligned Landsat-8 and Sentinel-2 images for all 60 SpaceNet-7 AOIs that are meant for training" / "least cloud cover" / "alignment accuracies are at the sub-pixel level" | The 15 m pan band is listed in Table 1 (p4, "8 15 m Panchromatic"), and the text calls it Landsat-8's finest band. The text does not explicitly say pan is a separate layer in every delivered stack. Low-risk, but "Landsat-8 (15-30 m)" is safer. ~24 months: "each temporal stack spans roughly 24 months" (p3). |
| 12 | Van Etten et al. 2021 (SpaceNet 7/MUDS, CVPR) | ~4 m PlanetScope monthly mosaics, 101 AOIs, ~24 months, building IDs persisting over time, 2017–2020 | SUPPORTED | 1, 2 | "medium resolution (4.0m) satellite imagery mosaics, which includes ≈24 images (one per month)" / "101 labelled sequences ... Planet Labs' Dove constellation between 2017 and 2020" | The paper says "Planet Labs' Dove constellation" (Dove = PlanetScope), not "PlanetScope". IDs: "Each building is assigned a unique identifier ... which permits tracking of individual objects over time" (p1). |

## Follow-up (Claude, 2026-10-04)
- **#11 MuRA-T panchromatic band:** confirmed from the dataset itself, not the paper text. Streaming the start of `landsat_murat.tar.gz` listed `coreg_LC08_L1TP_..._B8.TIF` (band 8, panchromatic) in each monthly folder, for example `train/L15-1703E-1219N_6813_3313_13/2018-06/`. The slides cite the dataset for this point.
- **Corrections applied to the deck and abstract:** #1a (34.1% is the DVL-Bench average QA accuracy), #3 (the 8-image limit is TEOChat's), #7 (Zuo: CVA-based iterative calibration plus self-consistency). #6b concerns DynamicEarthNet, which the deck doesn't mention. #10 TAMRF is cited as 2026, its volume year.
