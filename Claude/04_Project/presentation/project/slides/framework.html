Agent framework (04_01)

Six dimensions of the routing agent

Goal

Compare baseline, roads, power and rail routes between the exact route1_short endpoints; report length and USD 10/m cost.

Environment

Saved 03_01 GeoPackages, AGENTS.md rules, a 3,000 m study rectangle, measured in EPSG:32737.

Tools

geopandas, shapely, networkx Dijkstra shortest path, pandas; matplotlib and contextily for maps.

Agency

Agent cleans, nodes, repairs gaps and routes. Human fixed endpoints, 50 m gap and 500 m endpoint limits, and the rate.

Feedback

Stop with endpoint_too_far, no_path or empty_network. A failed check means revise, never relax a threshold.

Verification

An independent audit script, synthetic cases and reruns. Real-world claims need imagery or authoritative data.

Goal: compare four candidate routes between the exact endpoints of the shortened baseline, with the easterly point as the start, and report length and a classroom cost of 10 dollars per metre.
